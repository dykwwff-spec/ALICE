# ALICE
Nagaland tourism AI companion is an innovative, AI powered tourism website designed to promote the nature beauty, rich culture heritage, tradition festivals, and unique travel experiences of Nagaland . the platform aims to make enjoyable for tourists from across India and around the world .
The website act as a virtual travel assistant, helping visitors explore popular destination such as Kohima ,Dzukou valley , Khonoma green village , Mokokchung, and Mon . Using artificial intelligence , the companion can provide personalized travel recommendation based on user' interest budget ,travel duration , and preferred activities .Key features include an AI travel planner , an interactive destination map , information about the local festivals and cultural attractions, accommodation suggestion  , transportation guidance, and weather update. A local language translator can help tourist communicate with local communities , while an emergency assistance section can provide important safety information and emergency contacts . The platform will also include a local marketplace where visitor can discover tradition handicraft , local food , handmade products , souvenirs, and services offered by local businesses . This feature aim to support local entrepreneurs , artisans , tour gudes , and small business while promoting sustainable tourism. The main objective of the Nagaland tourism AI companion is the bridge the gap between tourist and local communities through technology . it combines artificial intelligence , cultural preservation, and digital innovation to  create a more accessible and personalized tourism experience . by promoting lesser know destination , encourage responsible travel and supporting local livelihoods, the project aims to contribute to the economic and social development of Nagaland .
  our vision is to make Nagaland a more connected , accessible and digitally empowered tourism destination while preserving its unique identity , tradition , and natural environment.
